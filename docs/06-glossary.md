# 06 - Glossary

Working definitions for the terms used across this repo. When a word has a local meaning at your company, write it down; shared language is infrastructure.

**Account-Based Marketing (ABM):** Treating target accounts as markets of one, with coordinated marketing and sales plays per account.

**AE (Account Executive):** The quota-carrying rep who runs the sales cycle from qualified opportunity to close.

**BANT:** Qualification framework: Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline.

**CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):** Fully loaded cost to acquire a customer.

**CDP (Customer Data Platform):** System that unifies customer data from multiple sources into persistent profiles.

**Champion:** The insider who sells for you when you are not in the room.

**CLM (Contract Lifecycle Management):** Tools and process for drafting, redlining, approving, and storing contracts.

**CPQ (Configure, Price, Quote):** System for building accurate, approved quotes from a product catalog.

**Dedupe:** Identifying and merging duplicate records.

**DSO (Days Sales Outstanding):** Average days to collect payment after invoicing.

**Economic Buyer:** The person who can release budget and say yes.

**Enrichment:** Appending third-party data (firmographics, contacts, intent) to records.

**Forecast categories:** Commit (will close), Best case (likely), Pipeline (possible). Definitions must be written down.

**GRR / NRR (Gross / Net Revenue Retention):** Revenue retained from existing customers, without (gross) and with (net) expansion.

**ICP (Ideal Customer Profile):** The firmographic and behavioral profile of customers who buy, stay, and expand.

**Intent data:** Third-party signals that an account is researching a purchase.

**iPaaS (Integration Platform as a Service):** Middleware for building and managing integrations.

**LTV (Lifetime Value):** Total expected revenue from a customer.

**MAP (Marketing Automation Platform):** System for nurture, scoring inputs, and campaign execution.

**MEDDPICC:** Enterprise qualification: Metrics, Economic buyer, Decision criteria, Decision process, Paper process, Identified pain, Champion, Competition.

**MQL (Marketing Qualified Lead):** A lead marketing deems worth sales attention, per agreed scoring/thresholds.

**Reverse ETL:** Writing modeled data from the warehouse back into operational systems (e.g., scores to CRM).

**SaaS:** Software as a service; the business model this whole repo assumes.

**SAL (Sales Accepted Lead):** An MQL the SDR team has accepted to work.

**SDR (Sales Development Rep):** The rep who qualifies inbound and prospects outbound.

**Speed to lead:** Elapsed time from lead creation (or routing) to first human touch.

**SQL (Sales Qualified Lead):** A lead sales agrees is a real opportunity, ready for an AE.

**Stage age:** Days a record has sat in its current stage; the simplest pipeline health metric.

**Survivorship:** Rules deciding which value wins when duplicate records merge.

**System of record:** The one system whose value wins in a conflict for a given object.
